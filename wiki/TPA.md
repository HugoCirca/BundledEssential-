# TPA

- `/tpa <player>` — send request to teleport to them
- `/tpa here <player>` — ask them to teleport to you
- `/tpa accept` — accept pending (also `/tpaccept`)
- `/tpa auto [on|off]` — toggle auto-accept (saved to `tpa.json`, also `/tpaauto` / `/tpahere`)

Requests expire in 30s. Cannot TPA to self. Tab suggests players + subcommands.
